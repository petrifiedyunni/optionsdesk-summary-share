CSP — still the core income sleeve

  • Universe: top 100 liquid US names
  • Entry: IV Rank ≥ 70, 7–14 DTE, delta 0.20–0.30
  • Exits: take profit at 50% of credit; stop at 2× premium
  • Sizing: max 5% NAV per trade, max 5 open, regime-scaled
  • Collateral: fully cash-secured; sleeve ≤ 80% NAV
  • Ops: auto-approve optional; STOP / PAUSE / PANIC on Telegram
Auto-exit does not unwind “shouldn’t have opened” trades. Only 50% / 2× marks fire a buy-to-close.

Sleeve E — earnings IV crush

  • Window: 1–3 days to earnings
  • IVR floor: 65
  • Skip: implied move > 25%
  • Default structure: directional vertical (put credit if bullish, call credit if bearish)
  • Short delta: ~0.22; wing ~8% away
  • Naked CSP: only if implied move < 15% and human replies YES NAKED
  • Exit: 50% / 2× in session; looser 30% target the morning after print
  • Scan: top 200 liquid names (or a pinned watchlist)

Long equity desk

Separate swing workflow. Codified and supervised, but dry-run only — live stock orders are not enabled.

  • Pre-filter: ≥3 of 5 liquidity / participation checks
  • Scorecard: EMA stack, RSI 50–65, MACD turn, volume > 1.3×
  • Setups: breakout, EMA pullback, MACD cross, trend continuation
  • Stop: max(EMA21, price − 1.5 ATR); targets 1.5R / 2.5R
  • Managed exits: scale-out at 1R / 2R + trailing stop in the paper book
  • Risk: 3% default, 5% hard cap, size = risk / stop distance

How the processes run

  • CSP: main.py --optionsdesk-live
  • Sleeve E: engine/sleeve_e_supervisor.py
  • Equity: main.py --live-supervised (dry-run)
  • VOLHARVEST: --volharvest-supervised (paper / Telegram)
Each sleeve is its own process and IB clientId. They do not start each other. Telegram STOP / PAUSE / PANIC still apply per engine.

Operating notes

  • Account is SGD-base / USD stocks; cash can print negative while NAV stays healthy (margin). That is not an options P&L issue.
  • CSP and Sleeve E can run on live TWS together via the clientId pool (OptionsDesk keeps 5; Sleeve E takes the next free id).
  • Illiquid after-hours option quotes are skipped at trade-build, not auto-filled.
  • The desk remains collateral-first: no CSP without full cash security inside the sleeve cap.
