1. Strategy Objective

The desk sells short-dated cash-secured puts to harvest elevated option premium. It is selective by design: no trade is placed unless volatility, market regime, contract quality, and account-level collateral checks all align.

Core idea: monetize rich implied volatility while keeping exposure fully cash-backed and rules-driven.

2. Universe Construction

  • Market: liquid US equities only
  • Research default: top 150 liquid names
  • Current live setup: top 100 liquid names
  • Ranking basis: average trading liquidity / volume

3. Entry Criteria and Filters

Filter Layer Rule Why It Exists
Volatility IV Rank >= 70 Only sell puts when option premium is relatively elevated versus recent history.
Earnings No earnings within 5 days Avoid jump risk and event-driven repricing close to earnings.
Option chain Chain must be available Trade is skipped if expiry/strike data is incomplete or missing.
Market regime Bull = full size Range = reduced size Bear / choppy = no trade Short-vol and short-put risk is only deployed when the broad tape is not hostile.
Capital check Collateral must be available Every position must be fully cash-secured and fit inside sleeve limits.

4. Option Contract Selection

  • Structure: short cash-secured put
  • Target delta: approximately 0.25
  • Accepted delta band: 0.20 to 0.30
  • DTE window: 7 to 14 days
  • Target tenor: around 10 DTE
  • Execution quality filter: reject if bid/ask spread is above 20% of mid

5. Sizing Framework

  • Max 5% of capital per trade
  • Regime multiplier: 1.0x in bullish tape, 0.75x in ranging tape
  • Max 40% of NAV allocated to the options sleeve
  • Max 5 open positions
  • Max 2 new trades per week
Position count is derived from available collateral. If the account cannot reserve the full CSP cash requirement, the trade is rejected.

6. Risk Management

  • Take profit: close at 50% of credit captured
  • Stop loss: close if premium expands to 2x entry premium
  • Assignment aware: positions are fully cash-secured
  • Monitoring: open positions are checked continuously during session flow

7. Operating Safeguards

  • Approval workflow available before order transmission
  • Duplicate trade prevention
  • Per-session trade caps
  • Emergency stop / pause / panic controls
  • Pre-session checks for connectivity, market data, and capital availability

9. Practical Notes

  • A meaningful number of candidate trades are filtered out before entry; the strategy is deliberately selective rather than always-on.
  • Backtest skip behavior shows a large number of exclusions from gap-related conditions, reinforcing the strategy's focus on avoiding unstable setups.
  • The desk is best described as a systematic income sleeve with explicit capital caps, regime gating, and collateral-first execution.

10. Long Equity Desk Criteria

The long equity desk is a separate swing-trading workflow built around trend, momentum, and volume confirmation. It is currently a paper / dry-run process, but the selection and sizing logic are fully codified.

Stage Rule Interpretation
Initial pre-filter Must pass at least 3 of 5 checks Fast first pass to eliminate weak or illiquid names before full signal scoring.
5 pre-checks Price > $5, volume spike > 1.2x average, not down more than 8% day-on-day, minimum absolute volume, positive day close Looks for tradeable names with live participation and no immediate breakdown behavior.
Extended liquidity filter 20-day avg volume >= 500k, price >= $10, dollar volume >= $5m Ensures the broader universe remains liquid enough for swing deployment.

11. Equity Signal Scorecard

  • +15: close above EMA21
  • +10: close above EMA50
  • +10: close above EMA200
  • +20: EMA8 crossed above EMA21 in the last 3 bars
  • +15: RSI(14) between 50 and 65
  • +15: MACD histogram turned positive in the last 2 bars
  • +15: volume ratio above 1.3x 20-day average
Score tiers: Strong Buy >= 95, Watch >= 80, Neutral >= 65, otherwise Weak.

12. Equity Setup Types

  • Breakout: recent EMA8/EMA21 cross plus strong volume expansion
  • EMA Pullback: price above EMA21 but still close to short-term trend support
  • MACD Cross: momentum inflection as MACD histogram turns positive
  • Trend Continuation: trend remains intact even without a fresh crossover trigger

13. Equity Entry, Stop, and Targets

  • Entry zone: current price plus/minus 0.5 ATR
  • Primary stop: max of EMA21 or price minus 1.5 ATR
  • Target 1: 1.5R from entry
  • Target 2: 2.5R from entry
The stop framework mixes trend structure and volatility, rather than using a fixed percentage stop.

14. Equity Sizing Rules

  • Default risk per trade: 3% of capital
  • Hard cap: 5% of capital per trade
  • Position size: risk budget divided by stop distance
  • Warning flag: if capital used exceeds 25% of account
This means the equity desk sizes positions off downside risk first, not off conviction alone.
